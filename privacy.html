Privacy Policy

Project Manager and Personal Manager are private desktop applications controlled by their owner.

Scope

This policy covers the applications' optional Google account integrations, associated local records and this public information site. The Google OAuth application is currently named Project Manager. Project Manager and Personal Manager maintain separate application records and account permissions. Using a common Google Cloud project does not by itself transfer one application's records to the other.

Google data accessed and its purpose

  • Account identity: email address, name and profile picture identify the account the user connected and prevent messages or records from being attributed to the wrong account.
  • Gmail: when enabled, message headers, message bodies and user-requested attachments support message search, order and delivery checks, receipt matching, subscriptions and other explicitly requested mail tasks.
  • Other selected Google services: calendar events, Google Docs, Google Sheets or YouTube information may be read for features the user enables. Granting access to one service does not automatically authorize access to every other service.
  • Mail changes: when a mail-management feature is explicitly enabled and the corresponding Gmail scope is granted, the application may perform the specific mail action the user authorizes. Connecting an account alone does not authorize sending, deleting or reorganizing mail.

Google data is used to provide the user-facing features described here. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and security

Google authentication is handled by Google. The desktop applications store their OAuth credentials in app-owned macOS Keychain entries; this website does not collect passwords, access tokens or refresh tokens. The applications can use refresh tokens to renew access while Google permits it.

Retrieved messages, attachments and derived records may be cached or saved locally for the requested features. Protected application storage is used where supported. User-requested exports or copies outside protected storage remain under the user's control. The user is responsible for securing their device and backups.

AI processing and other recipients

If the user enables an AI assistant or asks an agent to inspect selected Google content, the content needed for that task may be processed by the chosen local model or by the configured external AI service. This is subject to the user's selected sharing settings and authorization. The policy does not claim that all assistant processing is offline.

Google data is not shared with unrelated recipients or used for unrelated purposes. Human or agent access to selected data is limited to the assistance the owner requests or authorizes. Service providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Retention, disconnection and deletion

Local records are retained until the owner removes them through the relevant application or deletes their local copies and backups. Disconnecting an account or revoking its Google grant stops future authorized access; it does not automatically erase previously saved documents or receipts. The owner can separately remove retained local records and backups they no longer want.

Access can be revoked at Google Account connections. Google may also expire or revoke access. A revoked connection requires a fresh user authorization and is not bypassed.

Google API Limited Use

The applications' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website and contact

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, sign-in forms or advertising. The hosting provider may process ordinary web request information to deliver and secure the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

For support, access or deletion questions, contact the owner using the support email shown on the Google consent screen for this application. Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
